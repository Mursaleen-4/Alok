# Alok Agro Industries

Official website for **Alok Agro Industries** - Processing, trading, and services for Pakistan's agriculture.

## About the Company
ALOK AGRO Industries started in 2015 as a cotton ginning factory and oil mills in Tando Adam, Sindh. The group expanded to include an IRRI-6 rice mill and a high-capacity rice polishing facility at Port Qasim Industrial Area, Karachi. In 2026, the company commenced biochar production supporting ESG criteria and carbon sequestration.

### Operational Capacities
- **Cotton Ginning Factory**: 200 bales per day (Tando Adam)
- **Oil Mill**: 1,000 kg/day edible oil; 30 tons/day oil cake
- **Rice Mill**: 140 MT per day (IRRI-6 rice processing)
- **Rice Polishing Unit**: 240 tons per day (Port Qasim Industrial Area)
- **Feed Mill**: 10 MT per day dairy feed pellets
- **Biochar**: 1 MT per day scaling to 5 tons per day (ESG / carbon sequestration)

### Sister Entities
- **ALOKA Commission**: Agricultural commodity trading agents (Rice, Cotton, Oil cakes, Oil, Dairy feed, Feed ingredients, and related products).
- **ALOKA Agro Services**: Comprehensive agricultural industry services and trade promotion.

## Technology Stack
- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons**: Lucide React + Bespoke SVG Icons
- **Typography**: Fraunces & Plus Jakarta Sans
